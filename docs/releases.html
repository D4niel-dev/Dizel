Known limits

What is not ready.

These are the boundaries behind the preview label. They are not release notes for a finished version.

  • ×v1.6 weights are pending.The preview checkpoint is for evaluation; v1.5 is the model you can run.
  • ×Downstream benchmarks are not published.Only held-out BPB and validation loss are available.
  • ×2,048-token context.The app does not extend the model's window.
  • ×No vision path.There is no image encoder, image tokens, or multimodal projection.

Release policy

Gates, not a date.

The stable 1.6 release ships after six measured gates pass, not on a calendar date. The repository release policy is the source of truth.
