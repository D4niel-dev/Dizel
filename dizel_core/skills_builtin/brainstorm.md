Brainstorm around the brief below.

1. Restate the goal in one line.
2. Offer 8 distinct ideas — vary the approach, not just the wording. One line each.
3. Mark the three with the best effort-to-impact ratio and say why in a sentence.
4. End with the first concrete step for the strongest idea.

Prefer specific, buildable ideas over slogans.
