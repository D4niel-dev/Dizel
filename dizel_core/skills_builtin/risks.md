Analyze the plan or situation below for risk.

- List the risks that could materially change the outcome.
- For each: likelihood (low/medium/high), impact, early signal, and a concrete mitigation.
- Rank the top three by expected damage.
- Separate real risks from generic worries.
- End with the one check to run first.
