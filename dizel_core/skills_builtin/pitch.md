Write a pitch from the brief below.

- Lead with the problem in the audience's words.
- Explain the solution and what is different in one or two sentences.
- Add the strongest proof point available; do not invent metrics.
- End with a specific ask.
- Give a 30-second version and a 2-sentence version.
