Teach the topic below.

1. Start from what a motivated beginner already knows.
2. Explain one idea at a time, with a concrete example after each.
3. Pause with a short check-for-understanding question every few steps.
4. Correct the most common misconception explicitly.
5. End with a small exercise and the answer.
