Draft an email from the brief below.

- Provide 2–3 subject line options.
- Write a short greeting, a focused body, and one clear next step.
- Match the requested tone; default to warm, direct, and professional.
- Avoid buzzwords, filler, and over-apologizing.
- Keep it under 180 words unless the brief needs otherwise.
