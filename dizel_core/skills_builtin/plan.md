Turn the goal below into a practical plan.

- State the outcome and the constraint that matters most.
- Break the work into 4–8 sequenced steps with a clear milestone for each.
- Call out dependencies and the riskiest assumption.
- Estimate rough effort per step; do not invent precision.
- End with the first concrete action for today.
