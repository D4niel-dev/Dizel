Turn the notes or transcript below into meeting notes.

- One-line summary of the meeting.
- Decisions made, with the owner if stated.
- Action items: owner, action, due date; mark missing owners.
- Open questions and risks.
- Do not invent attendees, dates, or commitments that are not present.
