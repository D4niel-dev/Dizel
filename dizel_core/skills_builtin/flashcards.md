Create flashcards from the material below.

- One fact or idea per card.
- Front: a short question; back: the shortest correct answer.
- Avoid yes/no questions and overly broad prompts.
- Group cards by concept.
- Add 2–3 cards that connect ideas rather than only recalling them.
